# L-meta-29313 and formulas with one inverter

For the assigned eight-vertex graph, the minimum size of an unshared formula is 14 gates both with AND/OR alone and when one NOT is available. The posted problem asks for a strict saving with arbitrary sharing. That question remains open.

Reference.lean checks the 14-gate construction against the posted Boolean predicate on all 256 inputs. Its binary gates form a tree. The 13 minimal true and six maximal false inputs are in bounds.json. An independent integer checker verifies the upper and lower recurrence on all 524,288 pairs of subsets. The whole monotone formula requires 15 leaves, or 14 binary gates.

## Why one inverter cannot shorten the tree

Write the tree as a monotone context D with one hole carrying the complement of a monotone formula g. If g has k leaves and the rest of D has d raw leaves, there are k+d gates including the NOT. All eight essential variables occur outside the inverter. Along the path from the hole to the root, group the raw variables by whether they appear in an AND sibling or an OR sibling. A variable in both groups occurs at least twice. Consequently at most d−8 variables belong to both groups.

Triple.lean proves the following for any finite nonempty input type with n essential variables. If an antitone signal is used in one hole, the output is monotone, and d≤n+3, there is an equivalent monotone formula with at most d+2 leaves. On inputs where changing the hole matters, the monotone output agrees with the antitone signal. Moving inputs to a representative preserves the output and keeps only the coordinates in both groups. Opposite labels on these coordinates are incomparable. On three coordinates, a constant, a projection or a binary AND or OR of projections interpolates every such pair of label sets. This improves the earlier conversion for at most n+2 raw leaves.

If k≥2, a putative 13-gate formula would have d≤11, yielding a monotone formula with at most 13 leaves. If k=1, give the decoder the raw complemented bit as an extra input. Every such decoder needs at least 14 leaves even when it can use that bit repeatedly. Thus the original one-inverter formula needs at least 14 gates in both cases. The eight raw-literal proof modules are regenerated by rawcerts.py. Each matrix bounds the weight of every rectangle that a single leaf can separate. The same integer argument independently certifies at least 14 decoder leaves for each of the 28 inverted raw conjunctions and 28 inverted raw disjunctions. These latter bounds allow all raw inputs and the complemented signal to feed the decoder. checkweights.py verifies all 64 matrices with integer arithmetic.

## How expensive an interpolation can be

Realize.lean proves that every pair of opposite, mutually incomparable sets of cube points occurs as required labels for a monotone decoder with an antitone signal. Its construction uses the upward and downward closures of the prescribed samples. Cases that require many leaves therefore can occur in a decoder.

On five mixed coordinates, eight leaves always suffice for an interpolating monotone formula, and eight are sometimes necessary. cube5.cpp computes the exact price for all 16,606 maximal pairs of opposite label sets. Exactly 274 pairs attain eight. It checks all binary-operation inequalities and an attaining construction across the 7,581 monotone functions. One extremal pair has zero masks 13, 18, 19, 22, 25, 26 and one masks 7, 11, 14, 21, 28. These are independently reproducible integer computations.

## Shared gates

Across all 56 choices of one raw binary AND or OR followed by an unshared monotone formula, the total minimum including preprocessing is 14 for 18 choices and 15 for 38. The 18 choices are 17 ORs and the AND on vertices 3 and 7. In a different model, any number of shared OR gates may preprocess raw inputs before complete-bipartite products and a final OR. Here the minimum is also exactly 14. Only 4,653 product covers survive the initial 13-gate budget, and none has a sufficiently cheap OR construction.
