# Independent triples on six vertices

L-meta-18872 asks for a fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT circuit of size at most 42 on the fifteen edge indicators of a six-vertex graph. This directory contains partial results. Run `bash check.sh` with the repository's pinned Lean toolchain.

- `IndependentTriple18872.lean` proves that the 46-gate circuit computes the posted function on every input. Group the twenty triangle tests on missing edges by an owner edge. Six owners suffice, namely the six internal edges of a partition of the vertices into two triples. Dualizing the resulting monotone formula and appending one inverter gives the circuit.
- `TreeBound.lean` proves a 44-gate lower bound for binary formulas with at most one inverter anywhere. `SingleInverter.lean` also applies to circuits with sharing. When a circuit with exactly one inverter sends the all-zero input to true and the all-one input to false, its monotone suffix can be removed without changing the function. Its proof uses the upstream wire-monotonicity lemmas in L-meta-1018.
- `TriangleCertificate.lean` checks a signed rectangle certificate for the monotone triangle formula. The twenty triangle inputs are positive. The twenty-five complete bipartite graphs from cuts of type 2+4 or 3+3 are negative. Their matrix has total weight 260. Every rectangle certified by a single input coordinate has weight at most 6, including all subsets of its allowed rows and columns. The binary formula partition argument therefore requires at least 44 leaves.
- `SharpDesign.lean` proves the minimum cost of a factored triangle cover whose products generate only triangle monomials. Each group multiplies a linear factor by a sum of quadratic products. The three linear factors in any individual product admit only one edge each. Every cover needs at least six outer groups and twenty quadratic products. The minimum of the number of groups plus twice the number of quadratic products is 46, attained by the displayed construction.
- `design.py` independently checks both circuits on all 32,768 inputs and enumerates all 190 pairs of omitted triangle terms. The best 42-gate two-omission construction misses 708 true inputs and has zero false positives. The other two distances are 732 and 761. `reference.json` and `near.json` use the project's wire numbering, with `null` as the unused second operand of NOT.

The unrestricted 42-gate circuit remains open.

The independent full-truth-table search in `neighbors.py` checks 62,125 alternative single-gate replacements of the displayed approximation, including additional inverters and constants. Six tie the original error of 708 and every other replacement increases it. Starting from the same approximation, `python3 plateau.py 80` expanded 80 circuits reachable by error-preserving gate changes and scored 4,970,000 substitutions. The minimum stayed at 708. Another 131 discovered circuits remained unexpanded at that cutoff.
