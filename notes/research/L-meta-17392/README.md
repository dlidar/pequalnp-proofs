# Triangle detection with 22 gates

The assignment's search shard permits exactly two NOTs and six to eleven ANDs. The scripts concern restricted families. They give no verdict on an unrestricted 22-gate circuit.

Run `node owned.js` to enumerate all nonempty subsets of ten triangle terms. In this model each selected term is assigned to a selected owner edge, the other two edges are multiplied, products with the same owner are joined by OR and then multiplied by that owner. Distinct owner groups are joined by a final OR. For t terms and g used owners, this takes t+g AND gates and t−1 OR gates. Two unused NOTs and any required padding occupy the other slots. The enumeration checks the upper gate limits and permits padding with unused ANDs if necessary to reach six. Of 997 feasible nonempty families, exactly 30 attain the minimum 64 missed inputs. The supplied 22-gate circuit has 11 ANDs, 9 ORs, and 2 unused NOTs. Direct evaluation checks all 1,024 inputs. The scope is pure factored triangle-term subsets.

Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python pair_lp.py 1` and again with `7` to generate integer rectangle certificates for adjacent and disjoint edge pairs. The monotone formula decoder sees all ten raw bits and every Boolean function of the designated pair for free. The integer certificates checked locally gave total weights 2170799 and 2155592 with cap 100000 per allowed leaf rectangle. The local samples had 29 positive and 25 negative rows for the adjacent pair, and 28 positive and 26 negative rows for the disjoint pair. Thus the decoder requires at least 22 leaves, or 21 binary formula gates, in both cases. Spending two gates on a prefix depending only on that pair puts this construction above the 22-gate budget. The signed rectangle induction is in the parent commit's FormulaMass.lean. The integer calculations completed independently of the subsequent Lean replay. The complete project-specific Lean replay and axiom audit had no recorded verdict when these scripts were preserved.
